Where to Find Real Government Data

These are the actual official portals that publish verified government scheme statistics.

DBT Bharat
Real-time Direct Benefit Transfer statistics — total transfers, scheme-wise counts, ministry breakdown.
dbtbharat.gov.in
PMJDY Progress Report
Weekly updated Jan Dhan account statistics — total accounts, deposits, RuPay cards, zero-balance accounts.
pmjdy.gov.in
Ayushman Bharat Dashboard
Live KPIs — total cards issued, hospital admissions, empanelled hospitals, state-wise breakdowns.
dashboard.nha.gov.in
MGNREGS MIS Portal
100+ real-time reports — job cards, employment generated, expenditure, state and district level data.
nreganarep.nic.in
PM-KISAN Dashboard
Installment-wise disbursement data, beneficiary count, state-wise distribution of payments.
pmkisan.gov.in
data.gov.in (Open API)
India's Open Government Data platform. 100,000+ datasets. Free API with registration. Scheme-wise CSVs available.
data.gov.in
India Budget Portal
Annual Union Budget documents — scheme-wise demands for grants, ministry allocations, XLS downloads.
indiabudget.gov.in
NDAP — NITI Aayog
National Data & Analytics Platform. Standardized multi-sector datasets — agriculture, health, education, employment.
ndap.niti.gov.in
